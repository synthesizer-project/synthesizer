
Pre-Computed Grids
==================

Synthesizer was built on the ethos of being **flexible**.
With this in mind, we have generated a variety of grids for different SPS models and AGN models, under various photoionisation modelling assumptions. All grid files contain a complete summary of their model and photoionisation properties in attributes. The pre-computed grids are listed in the `Syndex catalogue <https://synthesizer-project.org/syndex>`_ and can be downloaded with ``synthesizer-download --dataset <name>``. SPS and AGN models as provided are listed under `incident`, and grids using our default photoionisation assumptions under `photoionised`.
